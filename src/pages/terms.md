The operator’s legal name, applicable jurisdictions, eligibility and parental authority, acceptable-use enforcement, content permissions, liability provisions, and change-notice process require review before publication as final terms. No effective date is assigned.

## What the app provides

Along the Way helps parents record homeschool learning, plan days, keep examples of children’s work, track optional Bible reading, and connect with other families. It is currently in TestFlight. Features shown on this website reflect the app in development, and individual test builds may differ.

Records reflect what a parent enters. Review your entries, targets, dates, and totals for accuracy. The app does not replace your responsibility for educational decisions or maintaining records appropriate to your family.

## State information

Built-in state guidance currently covers Missouri and Iowa. **It is a planning aid, not legal advice.** Instruction options and requirements may vary or change. Confirm the current official requirements that apply to your circumstances and seek qualified advice when needed. Progress in the app is not a certification that legal obligations have been met.

## Parent accounts and family access

Current accounts belong to parents. A family join code grants another parent access to the same family’s records. A village code instead connects separate families and does not expose their academic records to each other. Keep passwords, email codes, and join codes private from people who should not have the access they grant.

Joining another family with an existing account is a move, not a merger of family records. If you are the last parent leaving a family, the app can delete that family and its records after the required confirmation.

## Family records and shared photos

Academic records, plans, keepsakes, lesson photos, and reading progress remain within the owning family. A parent may separately choose to share a village photo. The app does not attach child names, tags, captions, or academic-record links to that photo.

**Proposed content expectations for review:** upload only material you are authorized to use and share; obtain appropriate permission from people depicted; do not share another person’s private information or harmful, unlawful, or infringing material. These expectations require the owner’s adopted policy and enforcement process before the terms become final.

Village photos are available for a limited period, and a connected family may save a copy. Removing a photo, deleting an account, or ending a connection cannot recall copies already saved on other devices. The current report action hides a photo from the reporting family; a broader moderation or appeals process is not promised by this draft.

## Bible reading

The optional reader includes the Berean Standard Bible (BSB) and King James Version (KJV), labelled in the reader. The text is bundled in the app; recording chapter progress uses the app’s server. Reading a chapter does not automatically record learning hours.

## Account deletion

You can start deletion in Settings. Your parent account is removed; family records remain if another parent remains in the family. If you are the last parent, the family’s live records and associated image files are deleted. Existing backups, service-provider records, and downloaded copies need the separate treatment described in the [draft privacy policy](/privacy/).

See [Delete my account](/delete-account/) for the exact steps. Removing the app from a phone does not delete the account.

## Availability and support

The app is under development. This draft makes no uptime, data-recovery, response-time, or permanent-storage commitment. Service availability, remedies, warranties, and any limits on liability need owner and legal review; none are supplied as invented standard clauses here.

For help, email **[support@alongtheway.cc](mailto:support@alongtheway.cc)**. Do not send passwords, sign-in codes, private child records, or photos in an initial support message.

## Coming later

Subscriptions and billing are planned rather than implemented. This draft establishes no subscription prices, renewal, cancellation, or refund terms. Child sign-in, shared calendars, and book logs are also planned; they are not described here as available features.
