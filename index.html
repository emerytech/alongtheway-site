Your memories.

Add dates, titles, and notes so you can remember the learning behind the work.

A VILLAGE, WITH BOUNDARIES

Your family’s records
stay with your family.

Your village is for connection. Your children’s learning records, keepsakes, and Bible reading progress stay inside your family.

Photos you choose to share with your village are separate from those records. The app adds no child-name fields, tags, captions, or links to a child’s record.

FAITH IN THE EVERYDAY

A little time
in the Word.

Open the built-in Bible, read together or for a child, and mark chapters as read. Choose the Berean Standard Bible or King James Version.

The reader is optional. Switch it on in Settings when it belongs in your family’s day.
