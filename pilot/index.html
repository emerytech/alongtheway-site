THE PILOT

Walk with us early.

A small, free pilot for homeschool families who don’t mind a few rough edges and are willing to tell us what they find.

Ask for an invite

The pilot runs through TestFlight, Apple’s app for trying apps before they reach the App Store. Places are limited while we make sure everything holds up.

Ask for a pilot invite ↗

Tell us the email address you use for your Apple Account. We’ll send a TestFlight invitation to it. Please leave children’s names and family records out of your message.

What you need

  • An iPhone or iPad. An Android version is being tested and is not part of the pilot yet.
  • The free TestFlight app from the App Store.
  • A few minutes now and then to tell us what worked and what didn’t.

What to expect

  • It’s free. The pilot costs nothing. Prices for the finished app are not announced, and you won’t be charged for taking part.
  • It’s unfinished. Things will change between builds, and something may break. Keep any record you’re required to hold in another place as well until the app is released.
  • Your records are yours. Your children’s learning records stay inside your family, in the pilot as in the finished app. Read the privacy policy and terms before you start.
  • You can leave at any time. Delete your account from Settings in the app, or see how to delete your account.

What we’d like to hear

Log a week of learning, plan a few days, make an hours record, and connect with another family if you know one in the pilot. Then tell us where you got stuck, what you looked for and couldn’t find, and what you’d miss if it were gone.

Email support@alongtheway.cc, or use Send Beta Feedback in TestFlight.

State guidance in the app currently covers Missouri and Iowa. It is a planning aid, not legal advice. Families in other states can still record time and set their own targets.
